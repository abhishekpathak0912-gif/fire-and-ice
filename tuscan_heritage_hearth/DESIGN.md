## Brand & Style

The design system marries classic Tuscan culinary tradition with the relaxed elegance of Kathmandu’s heritage dining. Evoking warmth, artisanal craftsmanship, and time-honored hospitality, the UI feels like a bespoke linen-bound menu paired with fine stone architecture. 

The aesthetic sits at the intersection of **Minimalist Editorial** and **Tactile Warmth**. It eschews modern tech-gloss and vibrant neon saturation in favor of muted earth, weathered stone, and unhurried typographic rhythm. The target audience comprises discerning local diners, international travelers, and lovers of artisanal Italian food seeking authenticity, understated luxury, and an evocative culinary atmosphere. 

Visual design pillars:
- **Earthy Restraint:** Natural materials (clay, olive groves, porcelain, aged brass) dictate the palette. No synthetic or hyper-saturated tones are permitted.
- **Editorial Typography:** High-contrast classical serif headings paired with clean, utilitarian body type evoke printed broadsheets and vintage Italian epicurean journals.
- **Organic Depth:** Contrast is achieved through subtle shifts in material tone (porcelain to warm stone) and hairline brass accents, rather than dramatic dropped drop shadows.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in the natural materials of an old-world Tuscan kitchen set against Himalayan stone. 

### Role Allocations
- **Primary Surface (`#F5F1E9` - Porcelain):** The canvas base for the application. Delivers an organic, warm reading experience far softer than sterile digital white.
- **Container / Secondary Surface (`#E5DED2` - Warm Stone):** Used for recipe cards, reservation panels, food category lists, and inset containers.
- **Elevated Card Surface (`#FFFFFF` - Crisp Linen):** Reserved strictly for high-focus floating surfaces, modals, and active cart drawers.
- **Primary Brand / CTA (`#354638` - Deep Olive):** Grounded, stately, and botanical. Used for primary buttons, active navigation markers, and grounding surfaces like hero banners and footers.
- **Secondary Accent (`#B65E46` - Burnt Terracotta):** Used selectively for wood-fired callouts, seasonal badges, culinary distinctions, and italicized editorial phrases.
- **Decorative Accent (`#C5AA79` - Antique Brass):** Framed dividers, hairline borders, star ratings, crests, and subtle architectural flourishes.
- **Primary On-Surface (`#292923` - Olive Charcoal):** High-legibility text tone carrying an organic warmth that prevents harsh black contrast.
- **Dark Inverted Sections:** Footers and atmospheric highlight banners employ `#354638` as the background, using `#F5F1E9` and `#C5AA79` for text and ornamentation.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the classical literary heritage of **EB Garamond** with the neutral, rational readability of **Inter**. 

### Typographic Expressions
- **Editorial Flourishes:** `headline-*` and `title-editorial` levels regularly utilize italicized cuts in `EB Garamond` to highlight Italian culinary terms (e.g., *L'Antica Tradizione*, *Fior di Latte*), rendered in Burnt Terracotta (`#B65E46`).
- **All-Caps Eyebrows:** Category kickers, origins, and ingredient categories utilize `label-caps` set in uppercase with tracking (`+0.12em`), providing an architectural anchor above serifs.
- **Menu Pairing:** Menu titles use `headline-sm` in Deep Olive (`#354638`), while ingredient compositions use `body-md` in charcoal (`#292923`) with subdued opacity (`0.85`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an open, relaxed grid inspired by broadsheet editorial publications and menu folios. Generous margins evoke luxury through intentional breathing room.

### Grid Systems
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column layout bounded by a max container width of `1280px`. Margins expand to `margin` (`3rem`), gutters hold at `gutter` (`1.5rem`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` outer margins and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `margin-mobile` (`1.25rem`) and `gutter-mobile` (`1rem`).

### Rhythm & Alignment
Vertical rhythm strictly applies increments of `0.5rem` (`8px`). High-prominence menu items, pairing suggestions, and cellar reserves leverage asymmetric multi-column spans (e.g., 5-column imagery against 7-column tasting notes) to avoid utilitarian e-commerce monotony.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through **Tonal Layering** and **Aged Hairline Rules**, rather than stark drop shadows.

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - Base canvas is Porcelain (`#F5F1E9`).
  - Recessed or secondary cards sit in Warm Stone (`#E5DED2`).
  - Active interactive cards sit elevated in Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Ambient Light Shadowing:** When floating surfaces require elevation (modals, fixed reservation bars, mobile drawer sheets), use warm, diffused umber shadows rather than grey cast:
  `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(41, 41, 35, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(41, 41, 35, 0.04);`
- **Antique Brass Outlines:** Separation between cards, table rows, and headers is achieved via `1px solid rgba(197, 170, 121, 0.35)`. Inverted dark containers utilize `1px solid rgba(197, 170, 121, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The shape system employs soft, architectural radii (`level 1`). Curves are kept gentle and restrained (`0.25rem` standard, `0.5rem` for large containers) to mirror stone masonry, parchment labels, and classic Italian terracotta tiles.

- **Small Interactive Units (Buttons, Inputs, Badges):** `4px` (`0.25rem`).
- **Cards, Panels, & Imagery:** `8px` (`0.5rem`).
- **Circular Forms:** Full rounds (`9999px`) are strictly reserved for icon-only action anchors, quantity steppers, and dietary seals. Pill buttons are prohibited to preserve architectural formality.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Olive background (`#354638`), crisp Porcelain text (`#F5F1E9`), `roundedness: 1` (4px). On hover, subtle tint shift to dark charcoal-olive (`#28352A`) with a subtle `1px` inner brass glow.
- **Secondary / Outlined:** Transparent background with a `1px` Antique Brass border (`#C5AA79`) and Charcoal text (`#292923`). On hover, fills with Warm Stone (`#E5DED2`).
- **Editorial Link:** Text-only in Burnt Terracotta (`#B65E46`) with a permanent `1px` underline offset by `4px`, displaying an italicized transition on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Wood-Fired / Specialty Badge:** Burnt Terracotta tint background (`rgba(182, 94, 70, 0.12)`), solid Burnt Terracotta text (`#B65E46`), `label-caps` typography, 2px border radius.
- **Ingredient Filter Chips:** Warm Stone background (`#E5DED2`), Charcoal text (`#292923`). When selected, transitions to Deep Olive (`#354638`) with Porcelain text (`#F5F1E9`).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background is Porcelain (`#F5F1E9`) resting inside Warm Stone panels, or White (`#FFFFFF`) on Porcelain base.
- Border is `1px solid rgba(197, 170, 121, 0.5)`.
- Focus state: `1px solid #354638` with an ambient glow of `rgba(53, 70, 56, 0.12)`.
- Floating labels use `Inter` at `label-caps` in charcoal-olive.

### Cards (Menu & Experience)
- Constructed with Warm Stone (`#E5DED2`) or Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1px` border of `#C5AA79` at 30% opacity.
- Pizza & dish cards feature photography framed with an inner inset border of `0.25rem`, reinforcing an editorial framing motif.

### Reservation & Table Steppers
- Segmented controls feature Warm Stone tracks with a sliding Deep Olive (`#354638`) indicator. Numbers and time slots are styled using `EB Garamond`.

### Inverted Footers & Banners
- Deep Olive ground (`#354638`) with hairline Antique Brass dividers (`#C5AA79`, 20% opacity). Typography uses Porcelain (`#F5F1E9`) for headings and antique muted gold for subtext and operating hours.
